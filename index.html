Why RaY World

Tamil-first, always

A content-priority ladder puts Tamil first across the homepage, ranking and filters — without making any language second-class.

A layered pipeline normalizes your query, searches movies, TV and people, widens to typed endpoints and applies typo tolerance — so you find it even when you misspell it.
